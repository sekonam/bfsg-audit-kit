**Read this precisely, because the RDG boundary in `PRODUCT-SPEC.md` § 5.2 depends on the
distinction:** it is now a plain public fact — safe to state, sourced, not legal advice — that the
authority has begun issuing fines and that Abmahnungen exist. It remains legal advice, and
therefore forbidden in any generated document or outreach script, to tell a specific recipient
that _they_ face a fine or an Abmahnung. `assertNoLegalClaims()` and the message rules in
`PROMOTION.md` § "Message rules" do not need to change. What _can_ change, if the operator judges
it useful, is a general, non-personalised line in outreach materials along the lines of "die
Marktüberwachungsstelle der Länder prüft seit Januar 2026 aktiv" — a sourced fact about the
regulatory environment, not a claim about the recipient. Any such addition should go through the
same RDG review as everything else customer-facing.

## 2. Compliance-rate data — consistent with, and adding to, what `PRODUCT-SPEC.md` § 4 already cites

- The DataPulse figure already in the spec (2,446 shops, end of 2024, ~1% fully compliant) is
  confirmed by multiple sources still citing it in 2026 — it has become the standard reference
  point industry-wide, which is itself a signal that no larger/newer study has displaced it.
- **Aktion Mensch, 2025**: in a widely cited test report, only **roughly one third** of examined
  online shops were accessible; **keyboard operability remained a core recurring failure** — this
  lines up with the manual-testing emphasis in `RUNBOOK.md`, since keyboard traps are exactly the
  kind of defect axe under-detects.
- A separate 1,000-site sample found **74.6%** fail on **colour contrast** most commonly — the
  single most mechanically detectable AA criterion, which is a mild negative signal for ticket
  size (an easy automated fix drags the median shop's defect count down, consistent with
  `PRODUCT-SPEC.md` § 4's note that the median shop needs "a handful of fixes, not a rebuild").

Net effect: **no revision needed** to the SKU C-is-the-core-SKU conclusion or to the prospect-score
weighting in `src/core/findings/score.ts`. The new data corroborates the existing calibration
rather than contradicting it.

Sources: [it-recht-kanzlei.de](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/faq-barrierefreiheit-online-shops-bfsg.html), [gepruefter-webshop.de](https://www.gepruefter-webshop.de/shopbetreiber-blog/2025/12/barrierefreiheit-im-onlinehandel-was-das-bfsg-fuer-webshops-und-apps-ab-2025-2026-bedeutet/)

## 4. Overlay tools — Germany-specific regulatory rejection, confirmed

**BIK (Barrierefrei Informieren und Kommunizieren) testing centres will not certify a site that
uses an accessibility overlay** — they state they cannot make a reliable EN/WCAG conformity
statement for a site relying on an overlay widget. This is a Germany-specific, testing-body-level
rejection distinct from the FTC's $1M fine against accessiBe for deceptive claims (confirmed, April 2025) and the finding that **38.5% of businesses sued over accessibility in the US already had an
overlay installed** (AudioEye 2026 analysis). None of this changes anything in this repo — overlays
were already rejected on the merits in `ROADMAP.md` § "Deliberately not built" — but it is now
backed by a Germany-specific citation worth having on hand if a prospect asks "why not just install
a widget".

Sources: search results for "accessiBe overlay lawsuit 2026 Germany", see conversation history;
[ratedwithai.com](https://ratedwithai.com/blog/accessibe-review-2026), [halfaccessible.com](https://halfaccessible.com/accessibility-overlays-ada-lawsuits/)

## 5. Noise in the search results, named so it is not mistaken for signal

A large number of the pages returned for "BFSG 2026" queries are SEO/content-marketing sites
(`xictron.com`, `wolpersweb.de`, `web-accessibility-checker.com`, `7aufeinenstreich.com`,
`onlinebarrierefrei.de`, `taylancetech.com`) publishing near-identical "BFSG-Checkliste 2026" /
"BFSG Bußgeld 2026" posts. Treat facts from these as **directionally useful but unverified** unless
corroborated by a primary source (a law firm, `dejure.org`, a vendor's own pricing page, or
`northdata.com`). This crowding is itself a data point for `PROMOTION.md` § 4: the inbound SEO
channel for BFSG-adjacent keywords is even more contested now than when that section was written.
